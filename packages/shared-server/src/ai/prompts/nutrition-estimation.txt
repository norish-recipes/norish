Estimate the nutrition of ONE serving of this recipe as written.

Work ingredient by ingredient:
- Turn each line into grams, using typical weights for pieces, spoons and cups.
- Use each ingredient as it is listed: dry pasta, rice, grains and legumes by their dry values, meat and fish as raw unless the line says cooked.
- Count oil, butter and other fat used for cooking or dressing in full; for deep frying, count about a tenth of the oil as absorbed.
- Leave out water and ice. Count salt only toward sodium.
- Add the ingredients up and divide by the number of servings.

Values per serving:
- Calories in kcal; fat, saturated fat, carbohydrates, sugars, fiber and protein in grams; sodium in milligrams.
- Saturated fat is part of fat and sugars are part of carbohydrates, so neither can be more than its total.
- Fiber is not part of carbohydrates here.
- Calories should come close to fat × 9 + carbohydrates × 4 + protein × 4 + fiber × 2. Saturated fat, sugars and sodium add no calories of their own.
- Give every value; use 0 only for a nutrient the recipe genuinely lacks.
