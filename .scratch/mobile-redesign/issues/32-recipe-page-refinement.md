# 32: Recipe page refinement

**What to build:** A native Recipe page with its media viewer and its motion. See `.scratch/mobile-redesign/spec.md` Part 6 ("Recipe page", "Web's motion, the floor") and the Recipe board.

**Blocked by:** 34

**Status:** open

- [x] The recipe page keeps the native tab bar; its bottom accessory reads Cook and opens cook mode, with the timer beside it while a Recipe Timer exists, minimising on scroll as on every tab; the label cross-fades on push and pop (Mike, 2026-11-16, final; the Home and Cook toolbar and the floating controls go, no Home button, `recipe-bottom-bar.tsx` deleted). On every tab the accessory's timer has no fill of its own and the action keeps its label beside it wherever it fits. Header items pinned while scrolling, no scroll-edge title bar.
- [x] Glass servings and portion steppers; the calorie card matches the canvas.
- [x] A hero tap opens a full-screen viewer of every image and video from the one tapped: pinch zoom, swipe down to dismiss, "n / m". The video fullscreen button works (it sits under the transparent nav bar today).
- [x] `expo-image` (via `expo install`) for every server image app-wide, memory and disk cache; a recipe's icons prefetched as the page opens.
- [x] No grey circle for a food without an icon: an empty slot only when another row in that list has an icon, otherwise none (unit test for the rule).
- [x] Motion: rolling digits for servings, amounts, nutrition and portions; step number → check scale-swap; ingredient check fade and strike; the double-tap heart pop; haptics on servings steps and check-offs.
- [x] Gates green, including mobile's lint, typecheck, test and `expo export --platform ios`.

## Comments

- Decided: the viewer is a component in an RN `Modal` (hero and step photos share it, fade-and-fly out on a swipe down or the close button); zoom and pan are gesture-handler + reanimated (no installed package ships a viewer), the pan offset clamped by `clampOffset` also while pinching. `expo-image` came through `expo install` (no release-age block); the Avatar uses its `asChild`; the widget photo download and local picker previews stay as they are (not server images). The page reads its icons once with `useIngredientIcons`; `PrefetchIngredientIcons` pulls each answered picture into the image cache. Every spring-or-fade move goes through `lib/motion/settle-to.ts`.
- The calorie card is the canvas's Nutrition card (big kcal, bar against 2,000 kcal, Protein/Carbs/Fat columns with reference bars): `DAILY_REFERENCE` lives in the recipe feature, two i18n keys in all 18 locales; the donut is deleted. Steppers are one `GlassStepper` (page, portions, editor, Add to groceries); a step that cannot move is disabled and gives no haptic.
- Deviations: ingredient amounts roll in their own column, so a wrapped line hangs under the name, as the Recipe board draws it (fixed 54 px amount column); the check "strike" is the instant line-through with an animated fade, as web's row is; `RollingText.keyFrom` was dropped (no caller used "left"), `rising` is used by `AnimatedNumber`. `useSheetOpen` is gone. The viewer is a `fullScreenModal` route (`recipe/[id]/viewer`) with `statusBarStyle: "light"`, so no app-wide `UIViewControllerBasedStatusBarAppearance` flag; a transparent modal cannot set the status bar (react-native-screens), so the page no longer shows through while dragging down.
- Seen on an iOS 27.2 iPhone 18 Pro Release build (own :3400): viewer (open, light status text, restored on close), step-badge number → check swap, a list with some foods iconed (empty slot for the rest) and one with all iconed, a list with none (no slot), icons and the hero photo from cache with the server stopped, Servings and Share sheets and the viewer in dark, Reduce Motion (earlier round), swipe-down. A tap in the step-photo viewer did not tick the step behind it. `timer-from-accessory.yaml` passed three runs in a row. Frames/PNGs in the scratchpad `t32/round2/`.
- Decided (2026-11-16, final): the recipe page keeps the native tab bar and its accessory reads Cook (`CookAccessory`, `cook` in the shell's one action table); the shell takes the recipe id from the path, so no feature imports another. `useMarkRecipeShown` marks the page shown on the screen's `transitionStart` (cleared on a closing one, restored on `gestureCancel`), and until then, or while the recipe loads or is missing, the accessory keeps the action of the screen below. The action is keyed by what it is and cross-fades (Reanimated `FadeIn`/`FadeOut` on the app's spring; Reduce Motion skips layout animations), tab to tab too. Cook mode, the editor and the media viewer hide the tab bar; sheets hide it through `useMarkSheetOpen`. `recipe-controls.tsx` is deleted; cook mode keeps its bar and `TimerPill`.
- Cook mode's `TimerPill` is one glass `Rectangle` (@expo/ui) whose frame springs (`lib/motion/native-spring.ts`) from the pill to the timer list and back, the countdown and rows crossfading inside; `useMorphFrame` grows the host to the list's room before the list is drawn and keeps it until the list has shrunk; Reduce Motion: the glass takes its size in one step, the content fades. Next drops its label when the pill leaves too little room. Sheet fields are one `SheetInput` (secondary variant, capsule, optional prefix/suffix word inside): Servings, the planned note, new/rename cookbook, add to cookbook, the grocery item, product search, price and currency, and the store's name and link; the store's aisle rows and add field use the same muted fill. Seen on the iPhone 17 earlier (round 6): the morph in cook mode frame by frame, those fields in light and dark; left as is: the aisle field under the keyboard after typing in another field first.
- The accessory's timer has no fill: ring, countdown and done dot on the accessory's glass, after a `bg-separator` hairline, a 44 pt tap target. Every action is one `AccessoryAction`: inline (minimised) it keeps its label when the measured label width plus icon, gap and 6 pt sides fit the room the timer leaves (`labelFits`, unit-tested), otherwise the icon; expanded, the label always shows.
- Seen on an iOS 27.2 iPhone 17 Release build (own :3400, frames in the scratchpad `t32/round7/`): the recipe page and the dashboard at the top and minimised with no timer, a short countdown, one over an hour and one done, light and dark (Cook keeps its label in all; Add recipe keeps it beside a short countdown and Done, and drops to its icon beside 1:59:xx); push and pop at 0.05 s: the fade starts with the slide and ends ~0.15 s later; a half back swipe cancelled (the list's action shows during the drag, Cook fades back ~0.5 s after it settles); the Recipes tab popping to the list; Groceries and back; entry from the library, Today, a cookbook, the Calendar's planned item and a cold deep link; the page's end clearing the bar; the editor; the Timers sheet over the page; cook mode from the accessory. `recipe-controls.yaml` and `timer-from-accessory.yaml` each passed three runs in a row.
- Not seen: a timer notification opening the page (a tap on the simulator's cover sheet opened nothing), Reduce Motion (no simulator toggle in reach), a long translation such as German, the media viewer with photos, haptics.
- Not seen: the heart pop (synthetic taps never read as a double tap: the viewer opened or nothing), a viewer pinch-then-shrink on the device, haptics (a simulator cannot play them), a video poster.
