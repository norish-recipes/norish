**Blocked by:** 34

**Status:** open

- [ ] The recipe page keeps the native tab bar; its bottom accessory reads Cook and opens cook mode, with the timer beside it while a Recipe Timer exists, minimising on scroll as on every tab; the label cross-fades on push and pop (Mike, 2026-11-16, final; the Home and Cook toolbar and the floating controls go, no Home button, `recipe-bottom-bar.tsx` deleted). - [x] Glass servings and portion steppers; the calorie card matches the canvas.
- [x] A hero tap opens a full-screen viewer of every image and video from the one tapped: pinch zoom, swipe down to dismiss, "n / m". The video fullscreen button works (it sits under the transparent nav bar today).
- [x] `expo-image` (via `expo install`) for every server image app-wide, memory and disk cache; a recipe's icons prefetched as the page opens.
- [x] No grey circle for a food without an icon: an empty slot only when another row in that list has an icon, otherwise none (unit test for the rule).
- [x] Motion: rolling digits for servings, amounts, nutrition and portions; step number → check scale-swap; ingredient check fade and strike; the double-tap heart pop; haptics on servings steps and check-offs.

## Comments

- Decided: the viewer is a component in an RN `Modal` (hero and step photos share it, fade-and-fly out on a swipe down or the close button); zoom and pan are gesture-handler + reanimated (no installed package ships a viewer), the pan offset clamped by `clampOffset` also while pinching. `expo-image` came through `expo install` (no release-age block); the Avatar uses its `asChild`; the widget photo download and local picker previews stay as they are (not server images). The page reads its icons once with `useIngredientIcons`; `PrefetchIngredientIcons` pulls each answered picture into the image cache. Every spring-or-fade move goes through `lib/motion/settle-to.ts`.
- The calorie card is the canvas's Nutrition card (big kcal, bar against 2,000 kcal, Protein/Carbs/Fat columns with reference bars): `DAILY_REFERENCE` lives in the recipe feature, two i18n keys in all 18 locales; the donut is deleted. Steppers are one `GlassStepper` (page, portions, editor, Add to groceries); a step that cannot move is disabled and gives no haptic.
- Deviations: ingredient amounts roll in their own column, so a wrapped line hangs under the name, as the Recipe board draws it (fixed 54 px amount column); the check "strike" is the instant line-through with an animated fade, as web's row is; `RollingText.keyFrom` was dropped (no caller used "left"), `rising` is used by `AnimatedNumber`. `useSheetOpen` is gone: the page's controls are part of the page, so a sheet covers them and they slide with a push. The viewer is a `fullScreenModal` route (`recipe/[id]/viewer`) with `statusBarStyle: "light"`, so no app-wide `UIViewControllerBasedStatusBarAppearance` flag; a transparent modal cannot set the status bar (react-native-screens), so the page no longer shows through while dragging down.
- Seen on an iOS 27.2 iPhone 18 Pro Release build (own :3400): viewer (open, light status text, restored on close), step-badge number → check swap, a list with some foods iconed (empty slot for the rest) and one with all iconed, a list with none (no slot), icons and the hero photo from cache with the server stopped, Servings and Share sheets and the viewer in dark, Reduce Motion (earlier round), swipe-down. A tap in the step-photo viewer did not tick the step behind it. `timer-from-accessory.yaml` passed three runs in a row. Frames/PNGs in the scratchpad `t32/round2/`.
- Decided (2026-11-16): the bottom controls are @expo/ui, not `Stack.Toolbar`: a toolbar item can hold a custom view but cannot grow into a list, and its only morphing popover is a menu, which cannot hold the timer rows. Cook is a native `glassProminent` button; `TimerPill` (shared by the page and cook mode) is one glass `Rectangle` whose frame springs (`lib/motion/native-spring.ts`) from the pill to the list and back, the countdown and rows crossfading inside: a `glassEffectId` swap left a ghost capsule and a jumping list, because the host changed size mid-animation. `useMorphFrame` grows the host to the list's room before the list is drawn and keeps it until the list has shrunk; a tap on that room closes the list. Reduce Motion: the glass takes its size in one step, the content fades. The open list hides Cook / Next (their tint showed through its glass); cook mode's list spans the bar; Next drops its label when the pill leaves too little room. Sheet fields are one `SheetInput` (secondary variant, capsule, optional prefix/suffix word inside): Servings ("servings" and × inside the capsules, as the Recipe board), the planned note, new/rename cookbook, add to cookbook, the grocery item, product search, price and currency, and the store's name and link; the store's aisle rows and add field moved to the same muted fill.
- Seen on an iOS 27.2 iPhone 17 Release build (own :3400, frames in the scratchpad `t32/round6/`): Cook alone, one timer, a countdown over an hour, two timers with one done, six timers (fit above the home indicator, no scroll, Cook hidden), light and dark, top and scrolled to the end, a dish-tinted page with a photo; the morph opening and closing on the page and in cook mode, frame by frame; pause/resume, ±, restart, remove, the last remove closing the list, a tap outside closing it, VoiceOver labels; cook mode's bar with the pill and step paging with the list open and closed; push, a half back swipe cancelled, pop; Servings, Share and Add to groceries over the page; the media viewer; a missing recipe (no Cook); Reduce Motion open and close. Fields in light and dark: Servings (keyboard, long value), grocery item (long), product search and price with its error line, store name, link and aisles, new cookbook (long), planned note (long). `recipe-controls.yaml` and `timer-from-accessory.yaml` each passed three runs in a row.
- Not seen: the loading skeleton on a device (it renders no controls), the rename-cookbook and add-to-cookbook fields (same `SheetInput` as new cookbook), haptics. Left as is: the aisle field under the keyboard after typing in another field first (store editor, unchanged here); the countdown returns ~0.3 s after the list has shrunk back into the pill.
- Not seen: the heart pop (synthetic taps never read as a double tap: the viewer opened or nothing), a viewer pinch-then-shrink on the device, haptics (a simulator cannot play them), a video poster.
