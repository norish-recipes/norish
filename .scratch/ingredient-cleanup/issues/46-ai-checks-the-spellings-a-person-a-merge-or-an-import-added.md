# 46: AI checks the spellings a person, a merge or an import added (once, at the first start)

**What to build:** Found by the audit of ticket 31's final run (2026-10-22, `m31c_post_on`). Seeded chicken breast holds the spellings "boneless" (5 lines) and "lbs boneless" (1 line). Neither was seeded. Both were created on 2026-10-09 in 0.25: a line like "boneless, skinless chicken breasts" was cut at the comma (rung 2's bare reading is "boneless"), and the fragment was merged into chicken breast.

- AI never reads a seeded food, so nothing ever looks at the spellings added to one.
- No word rule tells "boneless" (wrong) from "kipfilet" (right). Ticket 43 is closed for that reason.

**Counts on the copy (`m31c_post_on`, after the first start).**

- **Seeded foods:** 510 non-seeded spellings (`ingredient_aliases.seeded = false`) on 268 foods. 159 foods have 1, 53 have 2, and 56 have 3 to 9. In a sample of 30, "boneless" was the only clear wrong one, and "bloemkoolrijst" on cauliflower the only doubtful one. The rest are translations or line texts that name the food ("gerookt paprikapoeder", "olie om te bakken").
- **Own foods:** 251 non-seeded spellings that are not the food's own name, on 189 foods. A sample of 30 had no wrong spelling ("hamburgerbroodjes" on burger buns, "sterke espresso", "snoep nora"). Own foods are left out.

**The 6 lines on the two spellings.** Their texts are "boneless, skinless chicken breast(s)" with a note, cut or price ("…, cut into strips", "(about 1 lb.) (($5.79))"), and once "lbs boneless, skinless chicken breast (…)". They name chicken breast only through the comma cut. If the spelling is removed and they are resolved again today, rung 2 reads "boneless" again, finds nothing, and mints "boneless" for each owner. AI's pass then deletes it as not a food, and the lines name nothing. That is why this ticket relies on ticket 47's rule: a piece on the junk pile is dropped. "boneless" is dropped and the line is read as "skinless chicken breasts". Rungs 1–2 do not know that text on the copy, because only "boneless skinless chicken breasts" is a spelling. So it mints (its words may file it under chicken breast), and the step queues it for the AI pass (one more request; expected: merged into chicken breast). The lines never end up with no food.

**Fix (Mike, 2026-10-22: "we need this indeed, but only for manually added non seeded"):** a step run once, at the first start with AI on, right after the first-start cleanup (see Version). Each seeded food with non-seeded spellings gets one request. It lists only those spellings, never seed spellings, and asks: which of these name this food, which are not a food, and which name another food (with its name)?

- **Names this food:** kept.
- **Not a food:** the fold goes on the junk pile (ticket 47). The spelling is removed (`deleteCatalogueAlias`), and its rows are resolved again (`resolveAgain`, line text unchanged). With the pile, the remaining pieces name the food, or the line names no food (Junk Line).
- **Names another food:** AI's name is looked up through `findNamed`, like any AI name: an exact spelling is taken, and slips alone go to the second question. The spelling moves to the food found (`moveCatalogueAlias`), and its rows follow. If nothing is found, the spelling is removed as below.
- **Not this food, no other named:** the spelling is removed and its rows are resolved again, minting where nothing knows the text. The step queues the mint for the AI pass.
- **AI Changes.** Each removal or move is an AI Change with Undo, and the Undo is remembered. The kinds today (`AI_CHANGE_KINDS`: merge, parent, plain-name, not-a-food) do not cover this, so add one kind, `spelling`. Its Undo puts the spelling back on its food and points back the rows that named it, the way `not-a-food` keeps a deleted food's rows (`readDeletedFood`). For a "not a food" removal, the Undo also takes the fold off the pile.
- **Prompt.** A new administrator-editable prompt, named through `ai/runtime` (ADR-0016), lives beside the resolution prompts.
- **Version, and AI off (Mike, 2026-10-22: with AI off at the first start it asks nothing and is not marked done; it runs on the first start where AI is on).** The step has its own key in the seed state, `spellingCheckVersion` (a field on `IngredientSeedStateSchema` in `packages/config/src/zod/server-config.ts`, beside `cleanupVersion`), and runs at boot right after the cleanup (`cleanUpCatalogueOnBoot`), through `oncePerVersion`. With AI off (`isAIEnabled()`), its pass returns null and asks nothing, and `oncePerVersion` writes the version only for a non-null outcome, so the next start checks again. With AI on it queues its requests beside step 8's pass, queues the foods it mints for the AI pass the same way, and the version is written. `CLEANUP_VERSION` stays at 4. AI turned on while the server runs is picked up at the next start. The nightly sweep is not used: it would need its own "not checked yet" mark per food.
- **Cost.** 268 requests on the copy. At ticket 31's average (224,544 tokens / 148 reading requests, about 1,520 each), that is about 410k tokens. That is roughly 1.8 times ticket 31's whole first-start pass (163 requests, 232k), plus a few second questions (about 480 tokens each) and the step-8 requests for the mints it leaves. At ticket 31's pace (164 requests in 84 s), that is about 2.5 minutes.
- Docs: a release-notes line. The 0.26 upgrade note on the first-start cleanup says AI checks the spellings people and imports added to seeded foods, each change has Undo, and it costs this much. `CONTEXT.md`'s AI Change entry gains "removed or moved a spelling that does not name its food".

**Blocked by:** 47 (the junk pile and its piece rule)

## Implemented (2026-10-22)

- **Once-step** `checkSpellingsOnFirstStart(queue)` (`packages/shared-server/src/ingredients/spelling-check.ts`), `SPELLING_CHECK_VERSION` 1, through `oncePerVersion("spellingCheckVersion", …)` (new `IngredientSeedStateSchema.spellingCheckVersion`). `oncePerVersion`'s `pass` may now answer null: nothing is recorded, so AI off leaves the version unwritten. With AI on it lists `listSeededFoodsWithOwnSpellings()` (catalogue repo: seeded foods with an alias `seeded = false`) and queues them. Boot step `checkSpellingsOnBoot` (`packages/api/src/startup/check-spellings.ts`) runs right after `seedJunkPileOnBoot`.
- **Queue.** No new queue: `addSpellingCheckJob(ids)` (`queue/src/ingredient-review/producer.ts`) adds a `check-spellings` job to the ingredient-review queue with `IngredientReviewJobData.check: "spellings"`; the worker then calls `checkSpellingsOf` instead of `askAIAbout` per food (same `asking-ai:n/N` steps, detail `SpellingCheckOutcome`), and queues no icons.
- **Per food** `checkSpellingsOf(id)`: the non-seeded spellings not folded as the food's name, one request (`askWhatSpellingsName`, `ai/resolution/spelling-check.ts`, prompt `ingredient-spelling-check`, sections `Name: …`, `Parent: …` when set, `Spellings:\n1. …`). Schema `{ spellings: [{ number, kind: this-food | not-a-food | other-food, otherFood }] }`, all fields present; a spelling the answer leaves out is kept. `REQUEST_BUDGET_MS` and `onceMoreOnTimeout()` (now exported) as ticket 36. A failed request throws and the worker records the step `failed`.
  - not-a-food: `removeSpellingByAI(id, aliasId, { notAFood: true })`; other-food: `findFoodNamed(name, id)` (new export of `ingredient-resolution.ts`, `findNamed` with null readings), found → `moveSpellingByAI`, none → removed without the pile; a second question with no answer keeps the spelling.
- **AI Change kind `spelling`** (`AI_CHANGE_KINDS`). Undo shape `ChangedSpelling` (db-schema): the alias (id, text, fold, locale, owner), and for a removal each naming line's whole food list + join and the groceries/recurring pointing at it (`readChangedSpelling`). Listed under the seeded food; a move has `targetId`. Both guard under the tree lock: alias still on the food, not seeded, not undone before (`wasSpellingUndone`, by alias id), not the food's last.
  - Removal: one transaction records the change, `deleteCatalogueAlias`, and for not-a-food `addToJunkPile([fold], { reason: "not-a-food", aiChangeId })`; after commit `forgetSpellingRules()` and `resolveAgain(rows, { mint: true, ai: true })`, so a mint is flagged `ai-queued` and announced for AI's pass.
  - Undo: a removal re-inserts the alias under its id (`restoreSpelling`; text taken meanwhile → `spelling-taken`) and rewrites the recorded lines whose foods all still exist, and points the groceries back; a move moves the alias back while it is still on the moved-to food and not its last. The pile entry goes through `takeJunkOfAIChange` as for every kind. A seed pile entry ("boneless" on a real instance) is not the change's, so it stays.
- **`resolveAgain(rows, { mint, ai = false })`**: with `mint`, a line whose text resolves to null (all junk) now loses the heir's food. Only `removeVariants` (first-start cleanup) and this ticket pass `mint: true`; the delete and step 4 (`mint: false`) are unchanged.

## Review fixes (2026-10-22)

- **Undo and the pile.** Undo of a not-a-food deletion or of a spelling removal takes the folds it removed off the pile whoever put them there (`removedFolds` + `takeOffJunkPile` in `undoAIChange`), so a seed entry ("boneless") no longer keeps the restored spelling from matching. `takeJunkOfAIChange` is gone; `ai_change_id` is still written.
- **Undo leaves later edits.** `removeSpellingByAI` notes after its `resolveAgain` what each row then names (`noteSpellingRemovalLeft`, `ChangedSpelling.left`, by row id). `restoreSpelling` points back only lines and groceries that name no food now or still that; the grocery loop is shared with `restoreDeletedFood` (`pointBack`). The rows are read once (`findRowsNamingSpellings`, `readLineFoods`); `readChangedSpelling` reads only the spelling.
- **Groceries.** `resolveAgain` (with `mint`) keeps a typed grocery whose parts all name nothing and points it at no food, as a line; the "For Mike" note above on groceries keeping the heir no longer holds.
- **Outcome.** `SpellingCheckOutcome.checked.unchanged` lists a spelling whose removal or move came back undone or gone, logged.
- **Job.** A spelling check is the ingredient-review job (name `review`) with `check: "spellings"`; the worker picks its question once.
- `onceMoreOnTimeout(feature)` now lives in `ai/runtime/timeout-retry.ts`.
- **Undo of a "not this food" removal** whose rows minted the same text: the own food holding the fold, minted after the change and not named, parented or kept distinct by a person (`findMintHolding`), is merged back into the food (`mergeCatalogueIngredients`), and `restoreSpelling` takes the text already on the food as restored, its rows pointed at that spelling. Where a person chose that food, the Undo still refuses with `spelling-taken`. Tests: `spelling-check.test.ts` "on Undo, merges back the food its lines minted from the text" and "refuses the Undo where a person chose ...".
