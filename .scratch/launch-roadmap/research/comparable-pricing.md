# Comparable pricing (recipe / meal plan / grocery apps)

Researched 2026-11-26. Prices are USD unless stated; the US App Store storefront varies by region. Flags: [V] fetched from the vendor's own page or the App Store listing; [S] search snippet or third-party only; [OLD] older than 2026; [?] conflicting or unverified. App Store listings show IAP prices but rarely say which tier includes what, so tier contents come from listing text.

## Per app

**Paprika 4** [V paprikaapp.com, App Store]. Moved from one-time Paprika 3 to a Premium subscription (App Store: "Paprika 4 Premium" $14.99, discounted $9.99; billing period not stated). Site says one subscription covers all devices, family members can share it, 1-week trial, prices not shown on site. Free tier is limited (site gives 50 recipes, one grocery list, no sync, but its FAQ contradicts this [?]). The old Paprika 3 one-time prices ($4.99 phone, $14.99 to $29.99 desktop) are [OLD/S]. AI: ingredient substitutions via on-device Apple Intelligence only (no server AI, no cap).

**Mela** [V App Store macOS]. Free download, one-time "Mela+" unlock: $14.99 on macOS; iOS $4.99 to $6.99 [S/?]. Supports Family Sharing. AI: on-device ML importer, no paid AI.

**Crouton** [V App Store]. Free + Crouton Plus $24.99 one-time (Early Bird $17.99) + "Discover" $1.99/month or $14.99/year. AI photo/OCR "Smart Import" exists; the listing does not say which tier includes it (a third-party page says Discover [S]). Substitutions require Discover.

**Recipe Keeper** [V App Store, developer Tudorspan]. Free + one-time Pro upgrade $19.99 (a NZ storefront showed $39.99 [S/?]). Sold per platform [S]. No AI beyond OCR scanning. (Do not confuse with OrganizEat "Recipe Keeper", which is a yearly subscription at about $11.99 [S].)

**Plan to Eat** [V plantoeat.com/pricing]. One plan, no free tier: $5.95/month or $49/year, 14-day trial without card, 60-day refund. Household sharing in the one account. No AI mentioned.

**AnyList** [V anylist.com/complete, App Store]. AnyList Complete is annual only: $9.99/year individual, $14.99/year household (up to 6 people, per vendor FAQ [S]). Meal planning and recipe import sit in Complete. AI: only Siri integration; none sold.

**Samsung Food / Food+** (Whisk renamed Aug 2023) [V App Store]. Food+ $6.99/month or $59.99/year (UK: GBP 6.99 / 58.99 [OLD, 2024]). AI in Food+: personalized meal plans, recipe customization/nutrition tweaks, "Smart Cooking Mode". Free tier gets AI plans limited to 2 applications. No published AI credit numbers [?]. Samsung's pricing page returned 403. Samsung is also testing a separate "Galaxy AI Premium" at $4.99/month with credit tiers [S, unconfirmed, not Food+].

**Whisk**. Now just Samsung Food (see above) [S]; no separate pricing.

**Pestle** (Apple-only) [V App Store]. Pro $2.99/month, $24.99/year, lifetime $39.99 to $49.99 (listing inconsistent [?]). AI: on-device Apple Intelligence parsing/naming only.

**ReciMe** [V App Store, S]. Free: 5 smart imports per week, manual entry [S]. "ReciMe Plus" $29.99 to $59.99 on the listing; about $39.99/year [S]; 7-day trial. Plus: unlimited social-media/photo imports, nutrition, an AI assistant [S]. The vendor's pricing page returned no data. Import-count cap is the gate, not an AI-credit meter.

**Honeydew** [V App Store, honeydewcook.com]. Free tier; Premium $6.99/month, $39.99/year (listing also shows Plus/Pro at $19.99 to $59.99 [?]). Photo/screenshot/social capture needs Plus. Plus: unlimited storage, home sharing (up to 6 members, per site), ad-free. AI is the headline: meal planner, assistant chat, import, substitutions, recipe generation, cookbook translation. No cap numbers given.

**KptnCook** [V App Store]. Premium listed $5.99/month, $59.99/year in the vendor-sourced text; regional/discounted annual $26.99 to $47.99; EUR 39.99/year seen [S]; 7-day trial. AI assistant "Skippi" (tier not stated).

**Mealime** [V mealime.com/lower-prices is from 2021 [OLD]; App Store [S]]. Pro $2.99/month; annual plans phased out in 2021. Older reviews quote $59.99/year [OLD/?]. No AI found.

**Cooklist** [V App Store]. Cooklist Pro $5.99 to $9.99/month, $49.99 to $59.99/year (several price points, region/legacy [?]); 7-day trial [S]. AI: meal planner, AI grocery list, PantryScan video, AI Chef. Tier gating not stated.

**Tandoor (hosted)** [V tandoor.dev]. EUR/month: Free 1 member / 10 recipes; Basic 1.99 (1 member, 1,000 recipes, 5 initial AI credits); Standard 3.49 (3 members, unlimited, 1 GB, 5 initial credits); Premium AI 4.99 (10 members, 3 GB, 100 AI credits/month). Credit = per-recipe import; model-dependent (cheap models several imports per credit). Closest direct comparable and the only one with published AI caps.

**Mealie** [V docs, ElfHosted store]. Project has no first-party hosting; bring your own OpenAI/other key (docs: $5 deposit suffices). Third-party ElfHosted: from $1.29/app/month on a yearly Flex Pro (Flex starts $9/month for 4 apps); includes a text-AI allowance of unstated size; photo import and video transcription need your own provider key.

**KitchenOwl** [V kitchenowl.org]. No hosted/paid plan or AI listed; self-host only.

**Grocy** [V grocy.info]. No hosted offering; donations only; no AI.

## Takeaways

- EUR 2.50/month sits at the low end for recipe apps with sync (Pestle $2.99, Mealime $2.99, AnyList about $0.83 to $1.25/month household, Tandoor Basic EUR 1.99 to Standard 3.49). Mainstream subs run $4 to $7/month, so EUR 2.50 is a clear discount and plausible as a sync/hosting tier; Tandoor's EUR 3.49 for a 3-member household is the closest benchmark.
- Household sharing is bundled, not an upsell, nearly everywhere (Plan to Eat, AnyList, Honeydew up to 6, Tandoor 3 to 10 members). Do not charge per member.
- The one public AI-cap precedent is Tandoor: a few starter credits on cheap tiers and 100 imports/month at EUR 4.99. Copying that shape (a few free AI imports, then a monthly credit pool) is defensible; ReciMe's "5 imports per week" is the other pattern.
- Cheaper non-AI features (OCR, on-device parsing) are free in most apps. Photo/video import and generation are what incumbents (Honeydew, ReciMe, Crouton Discover, Samsung Food+) gate behind paid; none sells image generation or per-recipe nutrition AI as a paid line.
- An AI tier around EUR 4.99/month (Tandoor Premium AI) or EUR 3.99 to 5 is in line with market; going above about EUR 6 puts you at Plan to Eat/Samsung Food+ prices without their maturity. Price AI as credits, because model cost per import varies by model (Tandoor's FAQ makes the same point).
- Caveat: no vendor publishes cost per AI credit and several app-store tier mappings are unclear. The Mealime, Samsung Food+ and Paprika 3 figures are older than 2026.
