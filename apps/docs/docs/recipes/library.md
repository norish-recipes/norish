# Library

The Library on the start page holds your recipes and cookbooks in one list.
Choose **Grid** or **List** above it; your choice is kept separately for phone
and desktop.

- **Grid** shows large cards with a photo.
- **List** shows compact rows: one column on a phone or tablet, two on a
  desktop, and three on a wide screen. A long title wraps onto a second line
  in place of the description, and the facts and tags wrap below it, so
  nothing on the row is cut off.

![The Library as a list in three columns](/img/screenshots/library-list-columns.png)

A cookbook page offers the same two views.
