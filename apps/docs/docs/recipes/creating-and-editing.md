# Creating and editing recipes

**Add Recipe** opens the recipe form. Editing a recipe from its actions menu
opens the same form.

- **Ingredients** are free text, one per line, like `200 g spaghetti`. Norish
  reads the amount and unit as you type.
- **Steps** go one per row. Drag a row to reorder it.
- **Photos** go on the recipe or on a step. A step can have several.

## Headings with `#`

Start an ingredient or a step with `#` to make it a heading, like
`# For the sauce`. Headings are not numbered as steps.

![A step section heading written with #](/img/screenshots/editor-section-heading.png)

## Recipe links with `/`

Type `/` and a recipe name in a step to link that recipe, for example a dough
or a stock you keep as its own recipe. Readers tap the link to open it.

![The / recipe autocomplete open over a step](/img/screenshots/editor-recipe-link-autocomplete.png)

## Step ingredients with `@`

Type `@` in a step and pick an ingredient. The word goes into your sentence as
plain text, and the ingredient shows as a chip under the step.

![The @ mention autocomplete, with chips beneath the step](/img/screenshots/editor-mention-chips.png)

The **Link ingredient** picker under each step adds a chip without naming the
ingredient in the text, so _"add the spices"_ can carry all of them.

When the ingredient line has an amount, Norish asks how much this step uses.
Type the number and press Enter, or press Escape to use the whole line.

![The ask: an amount entry opens over a freshly attached chip](/img/screenshots/editor-amount-ask.png)

**Amount…** on a chip changes it later: half, a third, a quarter, or an exact
amount. On a line of five eggs, entering **3** makes the step read _3 eggs_.
Tap a chip to remove it.

![Amount entry on a chip beneath a step](/img/screenshots/editor-amount-entry.png)

See [Step ingredients](./step-ingredients.md) for how these show on the recipe.

## Choosing the thumbnail

The first image in the gallery is the recipe thumbnail. It shows at the top of
the recipe view.

To use a different image, select the thumbnail control on that image. It is
then at the front of the gallery. The other images keep their position. The
change is in the recipe after you write it.
