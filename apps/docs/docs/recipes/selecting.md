# Selecting recipes

Select recipes in the Library or on a cookbook page to act on all of them at once.

## Selecting

- Press and hold a recipe card.
- Or swipe a card left, or open its three dots, and press **Select**.
- On a computer, Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) a card. Shift-click
  selects every card between the last one you clicked and this one.

While you are selecting, a tap adds or removes a card. Cookbooks and recipes
that are still importing can't be selected.

Your selection stays when you search, filter or switch between grid and list,
so you can collect recipes from several searches. The count includes recipes
the current search hides.

![Selecting recipes in the Library](/img/screenshots/selecting-library.png)

## The action bar

A bar at the bottom of the screen shows how many recipes are selected and what
you can do with them. Press the cross or Escape to stop selecting.

- **Add to cookbook** files the recipes into the cookbooks you pick, or into a
  new one you name. Recipes already in a cookbook stay there once.
- **Delete** deletes the recipes after you confirm. Recipes you may not delete
  stay, and the confirmation says how many.
- **Remove**, on a cookbook page, takes the recipes out of that cookbook. The
  recipes stay in your Library.

![Adding selected recipes to a cookbook](/img/screenshots/selecting-add-to-cookbook.png)

Selecting works [offline](/offline). If someone else changed a recipe before
your delete reached the server, that recipe is kept and Norish tells you.
