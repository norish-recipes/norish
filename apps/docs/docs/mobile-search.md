# Search on iPhone

Tap **Search** in the tab bar. With the field empty, Search shows Recent searches
and Categories. With the keyboard down, it also shows Quick filters and Tags. Tap
a Category, Quick filter or Tag to see its recipes. **Favorites** and **4 and up**
follow your Hidden Items settings, as do Ingredient icons.

![Search with Recent searches, Categories, Quick filters and Tags](/img/screenshots/mobile-search-page.png)

## Search for recipes

Type in the field to see up to two recipe suggestions and **Narrow to** rows for
Ingredients, Tags and Categories. Tap a recipe to open it, or tap a **Narrow to**
row to add it as a filter and see results. Narrowing to chicken also finds recipes
that name a kind of chicken, such as chicken thighs. Tap **Search for** or the
keyboard's search key to see results for the text.

While the field has text, the Search Scope chips above it choose where to look:
**Title**, **Ingredients**, **Tags**, **Description** and **Steps**. Title and
Ingredients are on to begin with. At least one stays on.

![Recipe suggestions, Narrow to rows and Search Scope chips](/img/screenshots/mobile-search-typing.png)

## Results

Tap a filter chip's × to remove it. Use the sort chip to choose **Best match**,
**Newest**, **Oldest**, **A–Z** or **Z–A**. Tap **Filters** for the same filters
as the Recipes tab. Search's filters are separate from those on Recipes.

When searching, a line on each recipe shows where it matched, such as its title,
a Tag, an ingredient line, a step or the description.

![Search results with a Category filter, sort and match lines](/img/screenshots/mobile-search-results.png)

A search narrowed to an Ingredient shows the matching ingredient line.

![Search results narrowed to chicken](/img/screenshots/mobile-search-ingredient.png)

If nothing matches, Search may offer **Clear filters** or **Search all fields**.

![No results with Clear filters](/img/screenshots/mobile-search-no-results.png)

## Recent searches

Search keeps the last 10 confirmed searches on this iPhone for your Account and
removes them when you sign out. Tap a Recent search to run it again, or tap its
arrow to edit it first. Tap **Clear** to empty the list.

## Offline

Offline, Search shows Recent searches and what it last loaded. Searching needs a
connection and runs again when you reconnect.

![Search needs a connection message](/img/screenshots/mobile-search-offline.png)
