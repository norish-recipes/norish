# Calendar widgets on iPhone

Add Norish widgets to your iPhone Home Screen using the iOS widget gallery.
**Next meal** shows the current planned meal or the next one today. **This week**
shows today and the next six days. Tap a meal to open it in Norish.

![The Next meal and This week widgets on the Home Screen](/img/screenshots/mobile-widgets-calendar.png)

Widgets update when Norish runs: on launch, when it comes to the foreground,
and when it sees calendar changes while open. A change made elsewhere while
Norish is closed appears after you next open the app.

Widgets hold two weeks of plans and then show **Open Norish**. Signing out
clears their data and they show **Open Norish to sign in**.
