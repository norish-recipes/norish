# Households and permissions

Every account belongs to exactly one household. At sign-up, you start in a household of one. A household shares its groceries, pantry, stores and meal plan, as well as its [Content](/content) lists and ingredients.

## Join or leave

Open **Settings**, then **Household**. A household admin can share a join code. Enter the code to join from a household of one. If you already share a household, leave it first. A household admin must hand over the role before leaving.

When you leave, your recipes and cookbooks go with you into a new household of one. Copies of the Tags, Cuisines, Categories and foods your recipes use go with you. The household's groceries, pantry, stores and meal plan stay behind.

When you join, your groceries, pantry, stores, meal plan, lists and foods join the destination household along with your recipes and cookbooks.

See [offline changes](/offline#changes-made-offline) for what happens on your device when your household changes.

## Delete an account

Deleting your account erases the recipes and cookbooks you own. A planned meal whose recipe is erased becomes a note, and grocery items keep their text. If you are alone, your household is deleted too. If other members remain, household content stays and another member takes over as household admin if needed.

## Permission Policy

The server admin sets the Permission Policy under **Settings**, **Admin**, **Permissions**, then **Recipes**. It controls who can view, edit and delete recipes and cookbooks. Each level is **Everyone** or **Household**. Everyone means every signed-in account on the server. Household means the owner's household. View defaults to Everyone. Edit and delete cannot be wider than view. Server admins follow the same policy.
