# v0.23.1-beta

## Summary

A small follow-up to the grocery pricing that shipped in 0.23.0. The rule
that links a grocery to a shop product on its own was too loose. It is now
stricter, and forgives a typo.

## Fixes and Improvements

- A grocery is linked to a shop product by itself only when the product's
  name **is** the grocery's name. Before, a grocery named "snoepjes" was
  quietly priced as a bag of "Fortuin salmiak snoepjes", because that was the
  one product carrying the word. A product whose name merely contains the
  grocery's is now offered in the grocery panel, never taken.
- A name typed with a slip of the fingers still links: "sneopjes" is priced
  as "snoepjes". One wrong, missing, extra or swapped letter is allowed on
  names of five letters or more, two on names of nine or more. Shorter names
  have to match exactly, because one letter apart is a different word there.
- The product field in the grocery panel follows the same rule. Typing a bare
  word shows the shop's matching products to pick from; typing a product's own
  name fills it in.
- A grocery can be unlinked from its product. Open it and press **Unlink
  product**: the row loses its price, and the Store treats the name as one it
  has no product for until you pick or type one.

Groceries that were linked before this release keep their link. To correct
one, open the grocery and pick another product, or type the price yourself.
