# v0.25.0-beta

## Summary

## Features

### One catalogue of ingredients

Norish now knows that “onion”, “onions”, “onion, diced”, “ui” and “oignon” are one
food. Recipes and grocery lines still read exactly as written, but an aisle, a
product link, a store preference or a pantry item is now about the ingredient, so
you teach Norish each food once instead of once per translation.

- The catalogue starts from the Open Food Facts ingredients taxonomy, fetched
  before a new server first starts serving and refreshed nightly, so it already
  recognises thousands of foods in many languages.
- A new **Ingredients** tab in Settings shows the catalogue in your language. Each
  ingredient opens in a panel with its name, parent and translations. Norish flags the
  ingredients it was not sure about and says why; merge them into the food they are,
  mark them distinct, ask AI to sort them out, move a translation back out, delete
  one nothing uses, or say one food is a kind of another.
- **Ask AI** looks harder than an import does: a flagged name no known food shares
  a word with (“uien”, a brand name) is first named as the plain food it is, and
  compared with the foods that name finds. Its answer says what it tried.
- **AI suggests, you decide.** Ask AI and Find parent with AI never change an
  ingredient themselves: each answer is a suggestion (merge, parent, or a food
  of its own) you confirm or dismiss, one by one or all at once. A suggested
  parent is filled into the ingredient's panel, ready to save.
- Asking AI about every flagged ingredient on screen runs as one background job
  with a step per ingredient. It carries on after the tab is closed, every open
  Ingredients page shows each flagged row waiting its turn, and it shows in the admin job monitor with
  the models it asked. It asks a few ingredients at once, and when it
  ends the **AI suggestions** panel opens with what AI proposes for each, and
  why it had nothing for the rest.
- An ingredient's panel saves its name, parent and translations together with
  **Save**. The list is a tree of kinds: a food with kinds folds them out beneath
  it; a search or a filter lists flat. A filter shows the ingredients with
  neither parent nor kinds, and **Find parent with AI**, on one ingredient or
  all of those on screen, asks what food each is a kind of.
- Searching the catalogue finds the text anywhere in a name or translation; a
  filters panel beside the box switches to an exact match, searches the parent
  too, or shows only the flagged. The list loads more as you scroll, and
  flagged ingredients lead it.
- A kind of a food uses its parent's aisle and covers it in the pantry: “red
  onion” at home covers a recipe's “onion”.
- Server admins choose who may change an ingredient someone else added, under
  **Settings → Admin → Permissions**.

See [Ingredients](../groceries/ingredients.md).

## Fixes and Improvements

## Upgrade notes

- **Ingredients are merged into one catalogue on first start.** Every existing ingredient, Product Link, Aisle Link, store preference and Pantry item is carried over at startup; ingredients whose names differ only in case or accents become one. Nothing a household set up is lost.
- **New: `INGREDIENT_CATALOGUE_URL`.** Norish now seeds its ingredient catalogue from the Open Food Facts ingredients taxonomy, fetched at startup and nightly from GitHub. Nothing needs setting; on a server without internet, point it at a local mirror of the file or set it empty to turn the fetch off. See [Server & runtime](../configuration/server-runtime.md#ingredient-catalogue).
- **New: `INGREDIENT_REVIEW_CONCURRENCY`.** How many ingredients a round of Ask AI asks about at once, `10` by default. Lower it if a self-hosted model struggles with parallel requests. See [Server & runtime](../configuration/server-runtime.md#ingredient-catalogue).
