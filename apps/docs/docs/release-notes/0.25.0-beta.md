## Features

### Ingredient pictures

Ingredients can now have a small picture, so a line can be spotted at a glance
instead of read.

- Pictures show beside a recipe's ingredient lines (in cooking mode and on
  shared recipes too), on a step's ingredient chips, beside rows in the recipe
  editor, beside grocery items including ones typed by hand, and beside the
  names in your pantry.
- Administrators give ingredients pictures under Settings => Admin =>
  Ingredients, which lists every ingredient name your recipes use, with how
  many recipes use each. A picture can be uploaded, pasted, or drawn by your
  image provider in one shared style.
- A line shows the picture of the ingredient it points at, and nothing
  borrowed from a name that looks similar. A grocery item you typed by hand has
  no ingredient of its own, so it finds its picture by name — ignoring capitals,
  accents and punctuation, and nothing looser.
- An ingredient a recipe uses, or a household keeps in its pantry, cannot be
  deleted: Norish says what still uses it instead.
- The recipe editor suggests ingredient names your recipes already use as you
  type an ingredient line. Suggestions are optional, and any other text is saved
  as typed.
- Prefer text only? Hide **Ingredient pictures** under Hidden Items.

No ingredient has a picture to begin with. Existing recipes show pictures as
soon as they are added.
