# v0.25.0-beta

## Summary

## Features

### One catalogue of ingredients

Norish now knows that “onion”, “onions”, “onion, diced”, “ui” and “oignon” are one
food. Recipes and grocery lines still read exactly as written, but an aisle, a
product link, a store preference or a pantry item is now about the ingredient, so
you teach Norish each food once instead of once per spelling and language.

- The catalogue starts from the Open Food Facts ingredients taxonomy, fetched at
  startup and nightly, so a new server already recognises thousands of foods in
  many languages.
- A new **Ingredients** tab in Settings shows the catalogue in your language. Norish
  flags the ingredients it was not sure about; merge them into the food they are,
  mark them distinct, move a spelling back out, or say one food is a kind of
  another.
- A kind of a food uses its parent's aisle and covers it in the pantry: “red
  onion” at home covers a recipe's “onion”.
- Server admins choose who may change an ingredient someone else added, under
  **Settings → Admin → Ingredient Permissions**.

See [Ingredients](../groceries/ingredients.md).

## Fixes and Improvements

## Upgrade notes

- **Ingredients are merged into one catalogue on first start.** Every existing ingredient, Product Link, Aisle Link, store preference and Pantry item is carried over at startup; ingredients whose names differ only in case or accents become one. Nothing a household set up is lost.
- **New: `INGREDIENT_CATALOGUE_URL`.** Norish now seeds its ingredient catalogue from the Open Food Facts ingredients taxonomy, fetched at startup and nightly from GitHub. Nothing needs setting; on a server without internet, point it at a local mirror of the file or set it empty to turn the fetch off. See [Server & runtime](../configuration/server-runtime.md#ingredient-catalogue).
