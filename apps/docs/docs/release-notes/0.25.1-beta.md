# v0.25.1-beta

## Summary

A follow-up to 0.25.0: Recipe Keeper import, archive imports that keep much more of what their source app knows, a new app icon and favicon, and a handful of fixes to the calendar, settings, stores and recipe enrichment.

## Features

### Recipe Keeper import

Drop a Recipe Keeper export (`RecipeKeeper_….zip`) on the **Import recipe archive** card and every recipe lands with what it carries: courses and categories as tags, a course Norish recognises as a meal also as that meal, collections as Cookbooks, and the source, servings, times, Headings, notes, nutrition, every photo, rating and favourite.

A Norish recipe archive now names the cookbooks each recipe is in as well, and importing it files the recipes into your own cookbooks of those names.

## Fixes and Improvements

- Norish has a new app icon and favicon, and an installed app shows it again on every phone.
- Installing Norish from the browser shows screenshots of the app.
- The calendar opens on today again, instead of sometimes weeks in the past.
- Scrolling the calendar while offline no longer hides the plans it already shows.
- A setting changed while an earlier change was still saving no longer flips back for a moment.
- Stores keep a fixed order in the store manager, and a Store dragged past several others no longer snaps back to an earlier place.
- A recipe enrichment that finished while its page was opening no longer shows as in progress.
- A grocery's **Amount to buy** is the number the list shows for it, instead of 1.
- Archive imports keep their Headings: a "For the sauce:" line from Mela or Paprika, a Mealie section and a Tandoor header row arrive as Headings instead of as ingredients.
- Mealie imports read each ingredient as Mealie holds it now, linked to the ingredient Mealie named, keep which ingredients each step uses as Step Ingredients, and bring the favourite, cuisine, tools, notes and times along. A single recipe's Download zip imports too.
- Tandoor imports keep ingredient notes, lines without a food and "no amount", show templated steps as Tandoor shows them, and show waiting time as Other Time.
- A Mela recipe marked as a favourite is a favourite in Norish too.
- Recipes imported from an archive get Automatic Recipe Enrichment, like recipes from every other import.
- Two different recipes with the same name in one archive both land, instead of the second overwriting the first.

## Upgrade notes

- iOS keeps the icon and splash screen of an installed app. Remove Norish from the home screen and add it again to get the new ones.
