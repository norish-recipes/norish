# v0.23.0-beta

## Summary

**Grocery prices.** A Store can now point at a real shop, and Norish shows what
the things on your list cost there — as many packs as each line needs, what is
sold loose priced by weight, and the shop's deals as the shop presents them —
reading the shop's own pages, with no AI provider involved and nothing to pay
for.

**Aisles.** A Store can have the aisles of the shop it stands for, in the order
you walk them, and your list is shown by aisle. File a grocery once, by drag or
from its panel, and the Store remembers where that name is found, this week and
every week after.

**A calmer groceries page.** Every Store is a heading on the page with its rows
in a card beneath it, marked by a small dot in its colour rather than a
coloured box and an icon; ticked rows fold into one row that opens on tap.

## Features

### What your groceries cost

Point a Store at a shop by pasting a link you already have — its homepage, or a
search you just ran there — and Norish works out how to search it. You never
write a template, and the guess is shown to you rather than assumed: the
**Search Address** appears under the field with `{query}` marking where a term
goes, and the field stays yours to correct.

- A priced grocery shows its **Shelf Price** on the row with the shop's own
  words for the pack — `€2.99 · 150 gram` — and which of the shop's products
  that price is for.
- Adding a grocery **never waits on a shop**. A name your Store already knows is
  priced in the same breath; a name it does not goes to a queue, and the price
  appears the moment it lands — on your housemates' screens too.
- Norish links a grocery to a product by itself only where you would not
  hesitate. Anything less certain is left to you.
- Which product a grocery is, is a **field of the grocery's own panel**: it
  reads what the grocery is linked to now, searches the shop as you type, and
  drops down what came back with the price beside each one. Your choice is
  written when you press Save.
- A shop Norish cannot read is not a dead end: the field offers to take a
  price **by hand**, and nothing Norish reads will ever overwrite one you typed.
- Prices refresh themselves when they are more than twelve hours old and you are
  looking at the list.
- Every row carries a **Line Cost**: as many whole packs as its amount needs
  at the Shelf Price — `€5.98 · 2 × 500 gram` — with a bare number read as a
  number of packs, or as pieces when the shop counts the pack in pieces, so
  "12 eggs" against a box of ten is two boxes. What a shop sells loose is
  priced by weight, so 700 g of bananas priced per kilo costs seven tenths of
  it. A line Norish cannot work out still shows a price, with a quiet note that
  it counted one pack.
- The **Pack Size** Norish read — what one Shelf Price buys — sits under the
  product in the grocery's panel, and can be corrected there. A Pack Size you
  set is the last word and no reading overwrites it.
- A Store's heading adds up what is still to buy there: the Line Costs of the
  rows under it, in the shop's own currency. A row the Store cannot price is
  left out rather than guessed at. The grouped list prices a group as one
  purchase from its recipes' amounts added together.
- While the shop is being asked about a name, the row shows a loader where the
  price will go — on your housemates' screens too — so waiting never looks like
  failure.
- A **Sale** is shown as the shop presents it: the regular price struck through
  beside the Line Cost, a badge, and the shop's own words for the deal after the
  product name. A deal the shop keeps as words over its regular price, such as
  "2 voor €5.50", is shown in those words and never worked into the number.

For self-hosting operators: no AI provider is needed and no new environment
variables were added. Shop pages are read with a fixed ladder over the
structured data shops already publish for search engines, plus a pass over the
product cards for the prices. Store visits run on one always-on queue, one visit
at a time and paced, and there is no scheduled sweep — Norish never visits a
supermarket for a list nobody is shopping.

### Your list in the order you walk the shop

Give a Store **Aisles** in the store editor, under the colour: add one by
name, rename in place, drag to reorder, remove with an X. Nothing is written
until you press Save, the aisles are saved with the Store, and your household
shares them through the Store it already shares. A Store with no aisles is
exactly the Store it always was, and a Store with no website can have aisles
too.

- A Store with aisles shows **every aisle as a slim heading**, in the Store's
  order, whether or not anything is filed under it, a filled one counting the
  lines under it and an empty one quieter. The Store's heading, total, "Mark
  all done" and "Delete done" stay as they are, and ticked groceries fold into
  the Store's done row as everywhere else on the page.
- A grocery the Store has never been told about is **unfiled** and sits at the
  top of the block under no heading, where it is noticed and filed. Norish
  never guesses an aisle from words.
- **Drag a row into an aisle** to file it, back to the top to unfile it, or
  into another Store's aisle to move and file it in one gesture. In the grouped
  list, dragging a group files every name in it.
- **Or file from the grocery's panel**: an **Aisle** field under the Store
  selector, there only for a Store with aisles once the grocery has a name,
  pre-filled with what the Store remembers, swapping with the Store, and writing
  nothing until Save or Add.
- Filing is a fact about a **name** at a Store. Filing one "melk" files every
  "melk" at that Store, on every household member's screen, and the Store
  remembers it after the line is ticked off and gone: next week's "melk" is
  already in place. A renamed grocery follows its new name, a grocery moved to
  another Store follows that Store's memory, a removed aisle unfiles what was
  under it, and a deleted Store takes its aisles and memory with it.
- Groups in the grouped list are per aisle per Store: "kip" in Vlees and "kip
  (diepvries)" in Diepvries are two groups.

For self-hosting operators: no AI provider is involved and nothing goes
outbound. Aisles travel with the Store, so `GET /api/v1/stores` lists them
read-only; the REST create is unchanged, and filing has no REST endpoint. The
mobile app shows the list as it did.

### The groceries page, redesigned

The list reads as one calm page. Every Store, and Unsorted, is a **heading
directly on the page** with its rows in a card beneath it: a small
**dot in the Store's colour**, the name, what is left and what it costs —
"4 items · €17.86" — the chevron that folds the Store, and the menu that still
holds Mark all done and Delete done. The colour appears only where it marks:
the dot, and the **ring and fill of every checkbox** under that Store, so you
still know whose rows you are in once the heading has scrolled away. The
amount in front of a name stays in the accent, and rows under Unsorted keep
the accent too.

- **The icon is gone.** A shop is identified by its name; nothing you set is
  lost, and the eight colours a Store can have are now eight clearly distinct
  hues named by what they look like — Green, Rose, Teal, Amber, Red, Grey,
  Blue, Violet — the same on the web and on the phone, where two Stores that
  used to look alike no longer do.
- **Ticked rows fold into one row** at the bottom of the card that reads
  "3 done" and opens on tap, closed every time you open the list, so what is
  left to buy is all you see as you go. Untick something in it and it returns
  to its aisle.
- A Store where everything is ticked shows a **check mark in place of its dot**
  and says **All done**. A collapsed Store, and an empty one, is its heading
  line alone — and you can still drop a row on it.
- A row with nothing more to say is one line tall; the recipe a grocery came
  from, its recurrence or its product appear beneath the name only when there
  is one. Aisle headings are slimmer and count the lines under them.
- The By Recipe view takes the same shape, with each row's checkbox in the
  colour of the Store it is assigned to.

For self-hosting operators: the `icon` column on `stores` is dropped by
migration; a Store created through `POST /api/v1/stores` needs no icon, and
one that still sends it is accepted with the field ignored.

## Fixes and Improvements

- Grocery prices show the total and amount × price; a Sale reads like a
  shelf tag, the regular total struck through and the new one beside it with
  the shop's own words for the deal on hover, and the product name has its
  own line. The grocery panel links to the product's own page
  at the shop, and a Store is added or edited in a panel of its own over
  the store list, like every other editor. The grocery panel lets you adjust the amount to buy without
  changing the recipe requirement, keeps the recurrence control under the
  name, and opens the product's details in a panel of their own; a price or
  currency that is not one is said so before you save, and a product you
  correct by hand keeps its Sale and its pack. Counts now price correctly
  even when the shop has no pack-size information.

- **A grocery ticked after another had been added to its Store stayed un-done.**
  Adding a grocery moves every sibling in its Store down to make room at the
  top, and with it their versions; the screen that added it went on holding the
  old versions, so its next tick, rename or delete on any of those rows was
  refused as out of date and quietly undone by the refetch. The siblings now
  travel back with the add, and out to the household, at their new versions.
- **The image-generation queue appears in the job pipeline view** The admin job
  monitor's declared step sequence never listed it, so its jobs rendered without
  their steps.

## Upgrade notes

- Two new tables (`store_products`, `store_product_links`) and two new columns
  on `stores` are added by migration; `store_products` further gains the Pack
  Size and Sale columns, and `tried_at` on `store_product_links` becomes
  nullable. `groceries` gains a nullable `purchase_amount` column (migration
  0049). Two new tables, `aisles` and `aisle_links`, are added by migration 0050.
  Migration 0051 drops the `icon` column from `stores`. No environment
  variables changed.
