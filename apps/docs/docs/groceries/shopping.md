# Groceries & pantry

In **Groceries**, use **View mode** to group the list **By Store** or **By Recipe**.

An administrator can send your ingredients to Bring! instead. See
[Shop with Bring!](/groceries/bring).

## Add ingredients for planned meals {#add-ingredients-to-groceries}

Planning a recipe opens the groceries panel by default. It lists foods from
your [pantry](#pantry) separately. To add the ingredients automatically
or skip the panel, change **After planning a recipe** in account preferences.

## Pantry

Norish leaves foods marked **In your pantry** off the list when you add a
recipe. Use the box icon to add a food to the pantry. You can still add it
to your list by hand.

Use **Ingredients at home** to find recipes you have most of the ingredients for.

## Stores and aisles

Open **Manage Stores** in the list menu and edit a Store to add aisles. Type
an aisle name and press Enter. Drag the aisle handles to put them in shopping
order.

![The store editor with an aisle list](/img/screenshots/groceries-aisles-editor.png)

Drag a grocery to another store or aisle, or set its **Aisle** when you edit it.
Norish remembers the aisle for that ingredient at that store. Deleting an aisle
leaves its groceries on the list without an aisle.

## Prices

In **Manage Stores**, paste the shop's homepage or a search page into
**Shop link**. If the test finds no products when you save, check
**Search Address** and use `{query}` where the grocery name goes.

![A shop link and the search address derived from it](/img/screenshots/groceries-store-link.png)

Open a grocery to choose a product. Norish suggests a pack count. For example,
700 g of flour needs two 500 g packs. Use **Amount to buy** to change the count
or **Unlink product** to remove the price. The store total covers unchecked items.

## Clear the list

Choose **Clear list** from the list menu to empty the list. To empty just
one Store or recipe group, use **Clear all** in that heading's menu.
Choose **Delete done** to remove only checked items.

![A Store menu with Delete done and Clear all](/img/screenshots/groceries-clear-store-menu.png)

Clearing groceries cannot be undone. Your Stores and recipes stay in Norish.
