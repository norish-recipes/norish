# Pantry

The **Pantry** is the foods your household keeps at home: the olive oil, the
salt, the flour you never want to buy twice. When you add a recipe to your
groceries, the foods in your pantry stay off the list unless you tick them.
The pantry is shared with everyone in your household.

## The Pantry page

On the **Groceries** page, switch from **List** to **Pantry** at the top. The
Pantry has its own address, so you can bookmark it.

![The Pantry page, with olive oil on the list](/img/screenshots/groceries-pantry-page.png)

Each food is listed by its name in your language. Press a food to open its
[ingredient](./ingredients.md) panel, with its spellings, its parent and its
nutrition.

## Running out

When a food runs out, press **Add to groceries** (the cart) beside it. Norish
adds it to your grocery list, under the store you usually buy it at, and the
food stays in your pantry. While it is still to buy, the food shows **On the
list**, so your household can see it is handled. Ticking it off in the shop is
the restock: the cart comes back, and there is nothing else to do.

Only the same food counts: red onions on the list for one recipe never mark
your everyday onions as handled.

## Adding foods

Type in the field at the top. It narrows the foods you keep, by any of their
names in any language, and below them lists matching foods Norish knows, so
you pick exactly the food you mean. Press Enter to add what you typed as it
is, for a food Norish does not know yet.

### From your recipes

An empty pantry offers **From your recipes**: the foods your own household's
recipes use most, each with how many recipes use it. Press the plus to add one.
Once your pantry is filled, the list is folded away; open it any time.

![From your recipes on an empty pantry](/img/screenshots/groceries-pantry-suggestions.png)

### While adding a recipe

When you add a recipe to your groceries, the foods you keep are shown apart
under **In your pantry**. Press **We keep this** (the box) on any other line to
add its food to your pantry on the spot; the line moves under **In your pantry**
and stays off the list. A kept line that is already on the list says **On the
list**.

![The add-to-groceries panel, with We keep this on each line to buy](/img/screenshots/groceries-pantry-we-keep-this.png)

## Taking a food out

Taking a food out means your household no longer keeps it. Open the food's
panel, switch **In your pantry** off and press **Save**. The same switch is on the ingredient's
panel wherever it opens: on **Settings → Ingredients** and from a recipe's
nutrition, too.

![An ingredient's panel, with In your pantry switched on](/img/screenshots/groceries-pantry-ingredient-panel.png)

## Every spelling, every language

The pantry holds **ingredients**, not words. “ui” in the pantry covers a recipe
that asks for “onion”, and “onions” covers “onions, diced”, because Norish knows
they are the same [ingredient](./ingredients.md).

An ingredient that is a kind of another counts as that other one: “red onion” in
the pantry covers a recipe's “onion”. The reverse never holds: with “onion” in
the pantry, a recipe that asks for “red onion” still puts it on the list.

## What Norish does not do

- “salt” in the pantry is never mistaken for “salted butter”: they are two
  ingredients.
- The pantry is not an inventory: no amounts, no expiry dates, and no record of
  having run out. A food that ran out is simply on the list.
- Only adding a recipe to your groceries looks at the pantry. Anything you type
  on the grocery list yourself is added as usual.
