# Pantry

The **Pantry** defines what is already in the house. Groceries
listed in the pantry won't be added to the grocery list unless
explicitly marked.

## Filling your pantry

On the **Groceries** page, open the menu next to **Add Item** and choose
**Pantry**. Type a name and press Enter or the plus to add it, and press the
**X** to take one out. The pantry is case insensitive.

![The Pantry panel, with a few names in it](/img/screenshots/groceries-pantry-panel.png)

## Adding a recipe

When you add a recipe to your groceries, the ingredients you have at home are
shown apart under **In your pantry**.

![The add-to-groceries panel, with olive oil under "In your pantry"](/img/screenshots/groceries-pantry-section.png)

## Every spelling, every language

The pantry holds **ingredients**, not words. “ui” in the pantry covers a recipe
that asks for “onion”, and “onions” covers “onions, diced”, because Norish knows
they are the same [ingredient](./ingredients.md). The pantry shows each
ingredient in your language.

An ingredient that is a kind of another counts as that other one: “red onion” in
the pantry covers a recipe's “onion”. The reverse never holds: with “onion” in
the pantry, a recipe that asks for “red onion” still puts it on the list.

## What Norish does not do

- “salt” in the pantry is never mistaken for “salted butter”: they are two
  ingredients.
- Pantry items carry no amounts and no expiry dates.
