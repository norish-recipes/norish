# Planned recipes

When you plan a recipe, from the calendar, the **Today** row or a recipe's
**Add to calendar**, Norish opens **Add to Groceries** for it, so its
ingredients are one press away from your list.

Choose what happens under **Settings → User → Preferences → After planning a
recipe**:

- **Nothing**: planning only plans.
- **Open groceries panel** (the default): the panel opens at the recipe's
  servings, with every line to buy ticked and the foods in your
  [pantry](./pantry.md) left unticked.
- **Add ingredients to groceries**: the lines to buy go straight on your list,
  without the panel, and the foods in your pantry stay off it. If your pantry
  can't be read, the panel opens so you can tick what you need.

The choice is saved with your account, so it is the same on every device.
