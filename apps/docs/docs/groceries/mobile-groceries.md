# Groceries on iPhone

Open **Groceries** to see your household's list. Use the switch below the header
to choose Groceries or Pantry. Use **View mode** to group it
**By store** or **By recipe**. Tap a checkbox as you shop. Use the list settings to clear the list, or clear completed groceries from the
Done section. When the list has no items, an empty card appears.

![The grocery list grouped by store and aisle](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-list.png)

## Add and edit groceries

Tap **Add items**, enter a grocery and choose its amount, Store and repeat as
needed. Tap a grocery to edit it, including its Store, aisle, repeat and price.
Use **Repeat** for groceries you buy regularly. To delete a grocery, choose
**Delete** from the edit sheet's **Actions** menu.

![The add groceries sheet with a repeat and Store](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-add.png)

## Stores and aisles

Open **Manage stores** from the list settings to add, edit or delete Stores and
name their aisles. Tap **Add store** in the sheet header to add a Store. Hold a
Store or aisle to lift it, then drag to change its order. On the list, hold a
grocery to lift it, then drag to reorder it or drop it on a heading to move it
to that Store or aisle. Headings and other rows stay still while you drag. Norish remembers the aisle for that food at that Store.

![Manage stores with a Store's aisles](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-stores.png)

## Pantry

Switch to **Pantry** to see foods your household keeps at home. The search field filters the list. Every word must match part of a food name, in
any order, ignoring accents and punctuation, so "olive ex" finds "Extra virgin
olive oil". Tap **Add to pantry** to search the ingredient catalogue or add your
own. Catalogue results put exact matches first, then names with a word starting
with the first word you typed, using names in your language. When the field is
empty, **From your recipes** shows six foods with the number of recipes using
each. Tap **Show more** for the rest, or tap a food to keep it.

![Add to pantry with From your recipes suggestions and Show more](/img/screenshots/mobile-pantry-suggestions.png)
Tap a food to put it on the grocery list or remove it from the Pantry. Foods in
the Pantry are shown separately when you add a recipe to groceries.

![The Pantry with foods and Add to pantry](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-pantry.png)

## Offline

You can use the saved grocery list offline. Changes you make, including ticks,
edits and moves, are sent when you reconnect.
