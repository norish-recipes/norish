# Groceries on iPhone

Open **Groceries** to see your household's list. Use **View mode** to group it
**By store** or **By recipe**. Tap a checkbox as you shop. Use the list settings to clear the list, or clear completed groceries from the
Done section. When the list has no items, an empty card appears.

![The grocery list grouped by store and aisle](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-list.png)

## Add and edit groceries

Tap **Add items**, enter a grocery and choose its amount, Store and repeat as
needed. Tap a grocery to edit it, including its Store, aisle, repeat and price.
Use **Repeat** for groceries you buy regularly.

![The add groceries sheet with a repeat and Store](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-add.png)

## Stores and aisles

Open **Manage stores** from the list settings to add, edit or delete Stores and
name their aisles. Hold and drag a Store or aisle to change its order. On the
list, hold and drag a grocery to reorder it or move it to another Store or
aisle. Norish remembers the aisle for that food at that Store.

![Manage stores with a Store's aisles](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-stores.png)

## Pantry

Switch to **Pantry** to see foods your household keeps at home. The search field
filters the list. Tap **Add to pantry** to search for a food or add your own.
Tap a food to put it on the grocery list or remove it from the Pantry. Foods in
the Pantry are shown separately when you add a recipe to groceries.

![The Pantry with foods and Add to pantry](/img/screenshots/mobile-groceries-pantry.png)

## Offline

You can use the saved grocery list offline. Changes you make, including ticks,
edits and moves, are sent when you reconnect.
