# Manage ingredients

In **Settings**, open **Content**, then **Ingredients**. Search with parts of words in any order.
For example, "olive ex" finds "Extra virgin olive oil".

![The Ingredients catalogue](/img/screenshots/ingredients-list.png)

Open an ingredient to edit its name, add translations, or link it to a parent
such as "onion" for "red onion". Deleting an entry keeps the original text in
recipes and groceries. Check the confirmation before deleting.

## Correct nutrition

The Nutrition card on a recipe shows values per serving. If Norish calculates
them, the card lists any ingredients it left out.

Open **Nutrition** and select **Correct**. Pick a food from the database or
enter the values from the label. You can also correct the weight of a piece
or spoonful. Corrections apply to your household.

![Correcting the weight of a teaspoon of cumin from a label](/img/screenshots/ingredients-nutrition-correction.png)

## Change an icon

Select the ingredient's icon to **Upload a picture**, **Generate with AI**, or
**Remove icon**. Use **Hidden items** in your account settings to hide icons.

## Review AI changes {#ai-cleanup}

If your administrator has enabled [AI cleanup](../configuration/ai-provider.md#ingredient-catalogue),
open **Changed by AI** to review recent edits. Choose **Keep** or **Undo**,
including for ingredients that were merged or deleted.
