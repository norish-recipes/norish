# Clearing the list

When a shop is done, you do not have to remove the groceries one by one. You can clear the whole list, a single Store, or a single recipe's groceries in one go.

## Clear the whole list

Open the **settings** menu at the top of the **Groceries** page and choose **Clear list**. The entry only appears while the list has groceries on it.

Norish asks first, and says how many groceries will be removed.

![The confirmation for clearing the whole list](/img/screenshots/groceries-clear-list-confirm.png)

## Clear one Store

Every Store heading, **Unsorted** included, has a menu with **Clear all**. It removes every grocery under that heading and leaves the other Stores alone.

![A Store's menu, with Mark all done, Delete done and Clear all](/img/screenshots/groceries-clear-store-menu.png)

![The confirmation for clearing a Store](/img/screenshots/groceries-clear-store-confirm.png)

## Clear one recipe

In the **By Recipe** view, each recipe heading has the same **Clear all**. It removes the groceries listed under that recipe, including the **Manual Items** group.

![The confirmation for clearing a recipe's groceries](/img/screenshots/groceries-clear-recipe-confirm.png)

## Good to know

- **Clear all** removes everything under the heading, ticked or not. To remove only what you have already ticked, use **Delete done**.
- Clearing cannot be undone.
- Clearing a Store does not delete the Store itself, and clearing a recipe's groceries does not touch the recipe.
