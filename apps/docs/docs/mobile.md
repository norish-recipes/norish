# Mobile app

## Connect to your server

Enter your server address and tap **Connect**. Tap **?** for examples, such as
`https://demo.norish.app` or `http://localhost:3000`.

![Server address screen with a field and Connect button](/img/screenshots/mobile-server-address.png)

## Sign in or sign up

Choose one of the Sign-in Methods your server offers. To change your Server
Address, tap the server name on the sign-in screen.

![Sign in screen with email, password and server name](/img/screenshots/mobile-sign-in.png)

If your server allows sign-ups, tap **Sign up** to create an Account. Enter your
name, email and password, then tap **Create account**.

![Sign up screen with name, email and password fields](/img/screenshots/mobile-sign-up.png)

If **Sign-in failed** appears while the app waits for sign-in, tap **Try again**
to return to **Sign in**.

If **Storage unavailable** appears, the app could not read its saved data on
your device. Tap **Try again** to read it again.

## Update your server

**Update your server** means the server runs a version older than the app
supports. Ask your server administrator to update Norish. After the update, open
the app again or switch back to it. The app continues to **Sign in**, or to the
app if you were already signed in.

![Update your server screen showing the installed and required versions](/img/screenshots/mobile-update-your-server.png)

## Language and sign-out

Signed-out screens follow your device language. With a saved Server Address,
they use a language enabled by the server. On iOS, you can set a language for
Norish in the device settings. Once signed in, the app uses your Account language.

To end your session, tap **Sign out** and confirm. The app returns to **Sign in**
for the same server.
