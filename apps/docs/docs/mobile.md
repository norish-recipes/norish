# Use Norish on iPhone

## Connect to your server

Enter your server address and tap **Connect**. Tap **?** for examples, such as
`https://demo.norish.app` or `http://localhost:3000`.

![Server address screen with a field and Connect button](/img/screenshots/mobile-server-address.png)

## Sign in or sign up

Choose a sign-in method. To connect to a different server, tap the server
name on the sign-in screen.

![Sign in screen with email, password and server name](/img/screenshots/mobile-sign-in.png)

If **Sign up** is available, tap it, enter your details, then tap **Create account**.

![Sign up screen with name, email and password fields](/img/screenshots/mobile-sign-up.png)

If you see **Sign-in failed** or **Storage unavailable**, tap **Try again**.

## Update your server

If you see **Update your server**, ask your administrator to update Norish.
Open the app again when the update finishes.

![Update your server screen showing the installed and required versions](/img/screenshots/mobile-update-your-server.png)

## Shopping and calendar gestures

Hold and drag a grocery to a store or aisle heading to move it. In
**Calendar**, hold and drag a plan to another day to keep its meal slot.
Norish syncs changes made offline when you reconnect.

## Calendar widgets

Use the iPhone widget gallery to add **Next meal** or **This week** to your
Home Screen. Tap a meal to open it. If you changed plans on another device,
open Norish to refresh the widgets.

![The Next meal and This week widgets on the Home Screen](/img/screenshots/mobile-widgets-calendar.png)

For timers and sharing from other apps, see [Find and cook recipes](/recipes/mobile-recipe).

## Settings

Tap your avatar and choose **All settings**. Touch and hold a row to open its actions menu.

![Settings showing account, household, connections and app options](/img/screenshots/mobile-settings.png)

Tap your profile to change your name or photo, manage sign-in methods, change
your password, or delete your account. Tap **Save** after changing your name
or choosing a photo.

![Profile with name, sign-in methods and delete account](/img/screenshots/mobile-settings-profile.png)

Under **Preferences**, choose your language, measurements, phone display
options and hidden items. Set your allergies under **Allergies** and tap **Save**.
Set the app language in iPhone settings for use before signing in.

![Preferences for the account and phone](/img/screenshots/mobile-settings-preferences.png)

Under **Household**, see members or leave your household. Admins can remove
members, make another member admin, and copy, share or regenerate the join code.
Every account starts in a household of one. Leave a shared household to start a new household of one, then join another with a code. Your recipes and cookbooks go with you, along with copies of the Tags, Cuisines, Categories and foods your recipes use. Groceries, pantry, stores and the meal plan stay behind. A household admin hands over the role before leaving. Joining brings your groceries, pantry, stores, meal plan, lists and foods into the household you join. See [Households](/households).

![Household members and join code](/img/screenshots/mobile-settings-household.png)

Use [Content](/content) to manage your household's lists and ingredients.

Under **Connections**, manage site authentication tokens, API keys and recipe
share links. Copy a new API key when it appears. For an active recipe share link
created in 0.28 or later, touch and hold its row and choose **Copy link**.

![API keys with Create key and existing keys](/img/screenshots/mobile-settings-api-keys.png)

Under **App**, change the server or open support. Changing the server signs you
out and asks for confirmation. Tap **Sign out** to sign out. If you have
unsynced changes, confirm that you want to discard them before signing out or
changing server. The app and server versions appear at the bottom of Settings.

When Offline, you can read saved Settings data, but changes are disabled.
Sign out and changing server remain available.
