# Mobile app

## Connect to your server

Enter your server address and tap **Connect**. Tap **?** for examples, such as
`https://demo.norish.app` or `http://localhost:3000`.

![Server address screen with a field and Connect button](/img/screenshots/mobile-server-address.png)

## Sign in or sign up

Choose one of the Sign-in Methods your server offers. To change your Server
Address, tap the server name on the sign-in screen.

![Sign in screen with email, password and server name](/img/screenshots/mobile-sign-in.png)

If your server allows sign-ups, tap **Sign up** to create an Account. Enter your
name, email and password, then tap **Create account**.

![Sign up screen with name, email and password fields](/img/screenshots/mobile-sign-up.png)

If **Sign-in failed** appears while the app waits for sign-in, tap **Try again**
to return to **Sign in**.

If **Storage unavailable** appears, the app could not read its saved data on
your device. Tap **Try again** to read it again.

## Update your server

**Update your server** means the server runs a version older than the app
supports. Ask your server administrator to update Norish. After the update, open
the app again or switch back to it. The app continues to **Sign in**, or to the
app if you were already signed in.

![Update your server screen showing the installed and required versions](/img/screenshots/mobile-update-your-server.png)

## Recipes

**Today** shows your planned meals. To plan an empty slot, tap its add card and
choose a recipe, **Random recipe** or a note. Long-press a planned meal to move
or remove it.

![Today and the library in list view](/img/screenshots/mobile-recipes-list.png)

Under **Your library**, choose **All**, **Recipes** or **Cookbooks** and switch
between list and grid. Tap the filter button to narrow the library. Your view
and filters stay on this device.

Swipe a recipe card to add its ingredients to groceries, plan it or delete it.
Long-press for those actions, to favourite it or add it to a cookbook. Inside a
cookbook, you can also remove it from the cookbook. Double-tap a recipe photo to
favourite or unfavourite it. To plan a recipe, choose a day and meal slot.

When adding a recipe to groceries, adjust the servings and select the ingredients
to add. Ingredients already in your pantry appear separately.

### Cookbooks

Choose **Cookbooks** and tap the plus button to create one. In **New cookbook**,
enter a name, select recipes and tap **Create**. Open a cookbook to see its
recipes. Its menu offers **Add recipes**, **Rename**, **Change cover** and
**Delete**. To set a cover, choose **Change cover** and select or take a photo.
You can also long-press a cookbook card to add recipes, rename or delete it.

![New cookbook sheet with a name and selected recipes](/img/screenshots/mobile-new-cookbook.png)

### Add a recipe

Tap **Add recipe** and choose **From a link** or **From text**. If your server
has AI enabled, **From photos** lets you choose photos or take a photo. A copied
link can also appear in the sheet for you to import. Imports appear in the
library while they are processing.

![Add recipe sheet](/img/screenshots/mobile-add-recipe.png)

### Offline

You can browse saved recipes and meals offline. Changes such as plans, cookbook
edits, deletes and imports are sent when you reconnect. Uploading a cookbook
cover requires a connection.

## Share to Norish

Share a link, text or up to 10 photos from another app and choose Norish. The
Share Extension imports it without opening the app. If the server is
unreachable, Norish saves the share and imports it the next time you open the
app.

![Share Extension importing a recipe link](/img/screenshots/mobile-share-extension.png)

If you are signed out, your server needs an update or you share photos while AI
is off, the extension shows a message and **Close**.

## Language and sign-out

Signed-out screens follow your device language. With a saved Server Address,
they use a language enabled by the server. On iOS, you can set a language for
Norish in the device settings. Once signed in, the app uses your Account language.

To end your session, tap **Sign out** and confirm. The app returns to **Sign in**
for the same server.
