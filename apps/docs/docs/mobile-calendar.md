# Calendar on iPhone

Open **Calendar** to see the timeline around today. Scroll to another day. The title scrolls away with the days. When today is off
screen, tap **Today** at the bottom right, above the bottom bar to return. You can also tap the
**Calendar** tab again. Each day shows its planned recipes and notes by meal slot.

![The Calendar timeline with planned meals](/img/screenshots/mobile-calendar-timeline.png)

## Plan a meal

Tap **Plan recipe** in the bottom bar or the add button on a day. Choose a date
and slot, then select a recipe, **Random recipe**, or enter a note.

![The plan sheet with a date, slot and recipe choices](/img/screenshots/mobile-calendar-plan.png)

Tap a planned item to open its sheet. Change its date or slot, or edit a note,
then tap **Save**. Tap the recipe row to open it, or add its ingredients to groceries. To remove an
item, choose **Remove** from the header's **Actions** menu and confirm.

![A planned item sheet with date, slot and Save](/img/screenshots/mobile-calendar-item.png)

To move an item from its menu, choose **Move**, select a slot and tap the plus
button on a day card. Use the day strip above the cards to choose a day, then
tap **Save** in the header.

Hold an item to lift it, then drag it to another day. The day headings stay still. It keeps its slot and moves to the end of
that slot. You can browse saved plans and make changes offline. Norish sends
those changes when you reconnect.
