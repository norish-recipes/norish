# Calendar on iPhone

Open **Calendar** to see the timeline around today. Scroll to another day or tap
**Today** to return. Each day shows its planned recipes and notes by meal slot.

![The Calendar timeline with planned meals](/img/screenshots/mobile-calendar-timeline.png)

## Plan a meal

Tap **Plan recipe** in the bottom bar or the add button on a day. Choose a date
and slot, then select a recipe, **Random recipe**, or enter a note.

![The plan sheet with a date, slot and recipe choices](/img/screenshots/mobile-calendar-plan.png)

Tap a planned item to open its sheet. Change its date or slot, or edit a note,
then tap **Save**. From a planned recipe you can also open the recipe or add its
ingredients to groceries. Tap **Remove** and confirm to remove an item.

![A planned item sheet with date, slot and Save](/img/screenshots/mobile-calendar-item.png)

Hold and drag an item to another day. It keeps its slot and moves to the end of
that slot. You can browse saved plans and make changes offline. Norish sends
those changes when you reconnect.
